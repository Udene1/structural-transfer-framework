# Research Decisions

This file records durable methodological and project decisions so future contributors and agents do not repeatedly reopen settled questions without new evidence.

## DEC-0001 — Independent research identity

**Decision:** Keep this economics research programme separate from Cognitia.

**Reason:** The project must stand independently as economic research. Shared methodological ideas do not make the projects the same research programme.

**Status:** Active.

## DEC-0002 — Empirical neutrality

**Decision:** Do not begin from the conclusion that transferred economic policies are good, bad, suitable, or unsuitable.

**Reason:** The research question concerns transfer conditions and structural correspondence. The evidence must be able to contradict the initial hypothesis.

**Status:** Active.

## DEC-0003 — Domain similarity is insufficient

**Decision:** Treat similarity of domain, institutional form, policy objective, or terminology as insufficient evidence of structural equivalence.

**Reason:** The central research problem is whether underlying causal and evidence-generating structures correspond.

**Status:** Working principle; subject to empirical and theoretical refinement.

## DEC-0004 — Preserve uncertainty

**Decision:** Distinguish successful transfer, partial/conditional transfer, structural mismatch, transfer failure, and unresolved evidence rather than forcing binary conclusions.

**Reason:** Economic mechanisms may operate differently under different conditions.

**Status:** Active.

## DEC-0005 — Evidence before publication claims

**Decision:** Establish the relevant literature before claiming that the framework is novel.

**Reason:** Adjacent scholarship may already contain concepts that overlap substantially with the proposed framework.

**Status:** Active.
