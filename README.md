# Structural Transfer and Economic Policy

An independent research programme investigating when economic policy structures transfer successfully across economic environments, and when transfer fails because the underlying evidence-generating structures differ.

This repository is separate from Cognitia. It may share methodological ideas with that research, but it has its own questions, evidence, methods, state, and conclusions.

## Research programme

1. **Paper 1 — Structural Transfer and Economic Policy: A Framework for Testing Policy Fit Across Economic Environments**
2. **Paper 2 — Monetary Policy in Nigeria: Testing the Transferability of Inflation-Control Mechanisms**
3. **Paper 3 — Fiscal Federalism in Nigeria: Institutional Similarity versus Structural Equivalence**
4. **Paper 4 — When Economic Policy Transfers Fail: Cross-Case Synthesis**

## Core research question

Under what conditions can an economic policy mechanism developed or validated in one economic environment be transferred to another without producing structural mismatch?

## Working principle

Similarity of domain, institution, policy objective, or surface representation is not by itself evidence that the underlying structure is equivalent.

The research must establish what was transferred, identify the assumptions and mechanisms on which it depends, and test those structures against evidence from the target environment.

## Research standard

We do not begin by assuming that a transferred policy is good or bad, appropriate or inappropriate. The research should be capable of finding:

- successful transfer;
- partial or conditional transfer;
- structural mismatch;
- transfer failure; or
- insufficient evidence to determine the result.

Claims must be traceable to evidence and competing explanations must remain visible.
