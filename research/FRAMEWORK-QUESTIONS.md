# Framework Questions

These are research questions, not conclusions.

## Primary question

When a policy mechanism is transferred from environment A to environment B, what evidence is required to establish that the mechanism remains valid in B?

## Secondary questions

1. What exactly is transferred?
2. What structures does the mechanism depend upon?
3. Which structures are necessary, enabling, or incidental?
4. What source evidence establishes the mechanism?
5. What target evidence establishes that relevant structures are present?
6. What counts as structural equivalence?
7. What counts as structural mismatch?
8. Can a mechanism remain valid despite institutional differences?
9. How do we distinguish mechanism failure from implementation failure?
10. How do we distinguish transfer failure from an incorrect source model?
11. How should uncertainty be represented?
12. What evidence would falsify the framework?

## Provisional abstraction

Source: Policy P -> mechanism M -> outcome O.

Mechanism M depends on structures S.

Transfer to target B should therefore not be evaluated merely by asking whether P exists in B. We should investigate whether the structures and causal relationships supporting M in the source are also present in the target.

This abstraction is provisional and must be tested against existing scholarship.

## Alternative explanations

A different outcome in B does not by itself prove structural-transfer failure. Possible explanations include implementation differences, measurement differences, policy intensity, timing, shocks, source-model error, omitted variables, institutional constraints, political incentives, anticipation/adaptation, or genuine structural mismatch.

A valid framework must distinguish these.
