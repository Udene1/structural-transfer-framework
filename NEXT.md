# Next Research Action

## Current phase

Phase 0 — Empirical case reconstruction.

## Immediate objective

Test the proposed transfer audit against an actual temporal policy-reuse case while reconstructing competing causal mechanisms from evidence.

The first case remains:

**Nigeria monetary tightening, 2010–2011 versus 2023–2024.**

The research must not begin from the conclusion that monetary tightening either did or did not cause the later inflation decline.

## Completed research

The literature work established that:
- causal mechanisms are already studied in policy-transfer research;
- causal transportability provides formal source-to-target machinery;
- policy learning, evidence-informed policymaking, adaptive monitoring and economic diagnostics are established;
- the remaining candidate contribution must therefore be specific to the empirical operation of tracing mechanism requirements against changing target conditions and subsequent updating.

The empirical reconstruction has also established:
- 2010–2011 tightening was accompanied by contemporaneous diagnosis of inflation, liquidity, fiscal injections, FX pressure and expectations;
- 2023–2024 tightening was data/diagnosis driven and iterative;
- post-2023 Nigeria had materially different FX and transmission conditions;
- 2025 policy updating occurred alongside disinflation;
- none of those observations by themselves establish the causal contribution of MPR tightening.

## New empirical branch: food-market mechanism

A competing mechanism proposed during investigation is:

**high 2024 food prices → stronger production incentives → expanded planting/output → increased 2025 market supply → lower food prices → lower measured food inflation.**

This remains a hypothesis.

The first empirical pass found:
- NAERLS/FMAFS reports 2025 increases in production and cultivated area for major staples;
- area increases were generally modest;
- the current evidence does not establish increased farmer entry caused by high 2024 prices;
- a national high-frequency NBS/World Bank dataset exists with farmgate, wholesale and retail prices across Nigeria;
- imports are a serious competing supply channel, especially for rice;
- CPI rebasing means raw 2024→2025 headline comparisons require care.

## Immediate empirical operation

Use the NBS/World Bank High Frequency Crowdsourced Prices dataset for 2024–2025.

For maize, rice, garri/cassava, beans and other relevant staples, reconstruct:
1. 2024 farmgate prices.
2. 2025 farmgate prices.
3. Wholesale prices.
4. Retail prices.
5. Geographic dispersion.
6. Harvest timing.
7. Timing of price reversals.
8. 2024→2025 cultivated area and production changes where available.
9. Import timing and volumes.
10. Farmgate → wholesale → retail transmission.

### Core identification test

If increased domestic supply is materially responsible for a price decline, the strongest evidence should appear as a temporal/spatial pattern:

**new supply arrives → farmgate/wholesale prices weaken → retail prices follow**

rather than simply:

**national CPI falls → infer supply caused it.**

Then compare that pattern with:
- monetary-policy changes;
- FX stabilization;
- transport/fuel costs;
- imports;
- seasonal cycles;
- demand/purchasing-power changes;
- weather and security conditions.

## Farmer-incentive subtest

Separately search for direct evidence of the proposed behavioral response:

**2024 high prices → expected returns → planting/acreage/farmer participation decision.**

Do not infer farmer entry from aggregate output.

Evidence that would strengthen the mechanism:
- farmer survey responses linking planting decisions to expected prices;
- crop-specific acreage expansion;
- crop switching toward high-price crops;
- increased input purchases;
- production increases following the relevant planting cycle.

Evidence that would weaken it:
- little/no acreage response;
- output increase mainly attributable to yield/weather;
- imports explain the additional supply;
- price declines precede the relevant harvest;
- similar price declines occur in crops without comparable supply expansion.

## Required output before causal interpretation

Build an evidence matrix distinguishing:

**observation → proposed mechanism → competing mechanism → evidence → timing → confidence → unresolved question.**

No overall causal verdict until the physical-market evidence is reconstructed.
